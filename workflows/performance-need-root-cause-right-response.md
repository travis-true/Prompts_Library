# Performance need → root cause → right response

**Audience:** Workplace

> Use this workflow before committing to a course, guide, or campaign. It helps separate a knowledge or skill gap from process, tool, motivation, capacity, or policy barriers.


## Outcome

A concise problem statement, evidence summary, root-cause hypothesis, and recommended response with clear limits.

## Workflow

1. **Define the performance gap.** Describe the observable difference between current and desired performance.
2. **Identify who is affected.** Specify audience, role, context, frequency, and consequences.
3. **Collect evidence.** Use source documents, interviews, examples, metrics, support data, and direct observation.
4. **Test likely causes.** Check knowledge, skill, information access, process, tools, incentives, capacity, environment, and expectations. Record supporting and contradictory evidence for each hypothesis; a requested course is not proof of a learning gap.
5. **Select the smallest effective response.** Choose training only when learning is a material cause. Compare effort, dependencies, risk, and evidence strength. For uncertain causes, identify a small validation step such as an access check, observation, or sample review.
6. **Document assumptions and gaps.** Label facts, assumptions, inferences, and missing evidence.
7. **Confirm the recommendation.** Review the diagnosis with the request owner before production. Record the success measure, next action, confirmed owner, and what evidence would change the decision; leave missing ownership or dates unconfirmed.

## Response guide

- **Knowledge or skill gap:** practice, instruction, coaching, or assessment.
- **Information difficult to find:** job aid, QRG, checklist, or searchable reference.
- **Process unclear or inconsistent:** workflow clarification, SOP, or ownership decision.
- **Tool or access barrier:** technical remediation or support.
- **Expectation or incentive issue:** leadership communication or performance management.

## Quality check

- [ ] The gap is observable and measurable.
- [ ] Evidence supports the likely cause.
- [ ] Training is not treated as the default.
- [ ] The recommendation is proportionate to the need.
- [ ] Assumptions and unresolved questions are visible.

## Handoff

For an approved learning need, continue to [Minimum viable training package](minimum-viable-training-package.md) to select support assets, or [Training request → session blueprint → delivery → reinforcement](training-request-session-blueprint-delivery-reinforcement.md) when a live session is justified. Route technical or process barriers to their responsible owner.
