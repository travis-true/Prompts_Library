# Training request → session blueprint → delivery → reinforcement

**Audience:** Workplace

> Use this workflow for instructor-led, virtual, hybrid, workshop, webinar, demonstration, or blended sessions.


## Outcome

A delivery-ready session blueprint aligned to the performance need, audience, modality, time, evidence, and follow-through plan.

## Workflow

1. **Confirm the learning need.** State the business or performance outcome and what participants must do differently. If the need is unconfirmed, use the performance-diagnosis workflow before designing a session.
2. **Define constraints.** Capture audience, prior knowledge, modality, duration, technology, accessibility, and facilitator capacity.
3. **Write measurable objectives.** Use observable actions that can be practiced or demonstrated.
4. **Build the session arc.** Sequence opening, relevance, instruction, demonstration, guided practice, independent practice, feedback, and close.
5. **Create the run of show.** Assign time, facilitator actions, participant actions, materials, transitions, and contingencies. Include breaks and transitions in the total duration; match each objective to practice and observable evidence.
6. **Design evaluation.** Measure reaction only when useful; prioritize knowledge, skill demonstration, application, and operational outcomes.
7. **Prepare delivery assets.** Build slides, facilitator notes, participant materials, practice files, and support resources.
8. **Conduct QA and rehearsal.** Verify timing, accessibility, instructions, technology, and backup plans. Check task steps against current approved sources and try accessible participation alternatives. Record unresolved delivery blockers.
9. **Deliver and capture evidence.** Record completion, questions, difficulties, and performance evidence.
10. **Reinforce and maintain.** Provide follow-up practice, job aids, manager support, and review triggers. Confirm follow-up ownership and timing. Keep satisfaction, demonstrated learning, and later work outcomes separate.

## Minimum session blueprint

- Purpose and audience
- Performance outcome and objectives
- Modality and duration
- Agenda and timed run of show
- Practice and feedback plan
- Materials and roles
- Accessibility considerations
- Evaluation and reinforcement plan
- Risks, dependencies, and contingencies

## Ready-to-deliver check

- Every objective has matching practice, feedback, and evidence.
- The full run of show fits the scheduled duration.
- Source accuracy, accessibility, technology, and fallback checks are complete.
- Follow-up owners and unresolved blockers are visible.

Planning a session does not mean it has been delivered or evaluated.
