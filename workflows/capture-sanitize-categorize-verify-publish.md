# Capture → Sanitize → Categorize → Verify → Publish

**Audience:** Workplace

> Use this workflow whenever a draft, project artifact, or internal example may be valuable to a public audience.


## 1. Capture

- Identify the reusable idea, method, template, or example.
- Record its purpose, intended audience, and source.
- Separate the reusable method from organization-specific details.
- Confirm permission to reuse the source and preserve required attribution or license terms. Removing identifiers alone does not establish permission.

## 2. Sanitize

- Remove logos, internal links, proprietary terminology, and restricted instructions.
- Replace real people, email addresses, organizations, identifiers, and scenarios with clearly fictional examples.
- Use `example.com` for fictional email addresses and domains.
- Confirm that no confidential, member, employee, customer, account, policy, claim, or operational data remains.

## 3. Categorize

- Choose the correct library: Prompt, Skill, Workflow, or Training Asset.
- Add a concise description, audience, topic, format, platform, and complexity where applicable.
- Connect the record to related resources.

## 4. Verify

- Check the prompt, instructions, workflow, links, and downloadable files. Record what was inspected or tested and which checks remain incomplete; do not label a review as a runtime test.
- Check accuracy, usefulness, accessibility, and scanability.
- Review the page as a signed-out visitor when public access is intended. If that check is unavailable, record it as pending.

## 5. Publish

- Release only after required checks and owner authorization. The database’s Public label controls its filtered view; it does not establish page permissions. Confirm actual sharing separately, and change sharing only when authorized.
- Confirm it appears in the intended public view.
- Recheck the live page for accidental internal-content exposure.
- Record a maintenance owner, review trigger, and correction or withdrawal path for later problems.

## Release rule

If any source detail cannot be confirmed as public-safe, keep the record outside the public database until it is fully sanitized.
