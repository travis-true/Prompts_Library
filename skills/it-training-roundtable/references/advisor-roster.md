# Advisor roster

| Perspective | Bring it in when | Question and source |
|---|---|---|
| Cathy Moore, performance consulting | A stakeholder requests training or employees repeat an error | What must people do on the job, and what evidence distinguishes a skill gap from access, tools, incentives, or process barriers? [Action Mapping](https://blog.cathy-moore.com/action-mapping/) |
| Karl Kapp, practice design | A walkthrough or knowledge check needs a meaningful decision | What realistic choice can learners make, what happens next, and what feedback helps them try again? [Action-First Learning](https://assets.td.org/m/4b273f39f2a49c2a/original/1512506649_PubsCat-Spring25_digital.pdf) |
| Will Thalheimer, evaluation and transfer | An assessment or follow-up needs stronger evidence | What observable task shows independent competence, and what later evidence would show job transfer? [Learning-Transfer Evaluation Model](https://www.worklearning.com/ltem/) |
| Sarah Mercier, accessible learning design | A workbook, lab, or live session needs an accessibility review | Where do instructions rely on color, hearing, screenshots, or a mouse, and how can learners reach the same outcome? [Design for All Learners](https://www.td.org/product/book--design-for-all-learners/112501) |
| Ruth Clark and Richard Mayer, multimedia instruction | A software demo or microvideo needs editing | What should be highlighted, segmented, or trimmed to make the task easier to follow? [e-Learning and the Science of Instruction](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/e-learning-and-the-science-of-instruction-proven-guidelines-for-consumers-and-designers-of-multimedia-learning-5th-edition-p-9781394177370) |
| Bob Mosher and Conrad Gottfredson, performance support | Employees need help while doing a task or troubleshooting | What is the shortest usable decision aid, and where will employees find it at the moment of need? [The 5 Moments of Need](https://www.5momentsofneed.com/our-team/) |
| Tenant admin, local product verification | A rollout depends on account, app, license, permissions, or configuration | Who has the feature today, in which app, and what test confirms the steps? |
| IT Service Desk lead, workplace evidence | Ticket patterns or escalation paths may affect the decision | What keeps recurring, who is affected, and which problems point to access or process rather than training? |
