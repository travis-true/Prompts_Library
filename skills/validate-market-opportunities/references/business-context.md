## Eligible workplace markets

## Business boundaries

- Keep commercial work separate from employer work.
- Use only independently created, public-domain, or properly licensed material.
- Do not use employer artifacts, wording, examples, branding, data, templates, or proprietary processes.
- Use branding only when supplied and authorized.
- Keep corporate offers industry-neutral unless the brief provides an authorized specialization.

## Channel baseline

Record actual traffic, previews, buyer intent, checkout behavior, conversion, sales, and prior experiments separately for each channel. Marketplace discovery and owned-site traffic require separate diagnosis. Unknown values remain unknown.

## Decision preferences

- Weight buyer intent more heavily than buzz.
- Use 30-day momentum, 90-day validation, and 12-month context where evidence permits.
- Rank three opportunities per requested workplace niche and the top opportunities overall.
- Keep unsupported but meaningful ideas in a clearly labeled backlog.
