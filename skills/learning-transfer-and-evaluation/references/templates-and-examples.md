# Templates and examples

## One-page transfer plan

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Workplace behavior | [Audience] does [observable action] at [work trigger] to [standard]. |
| Intended outcome | [Operational result and proposed indicator]. |
| Application and follow-up | [Work opportunity], [reminder or check-in], [owner], [timing], [fallback if no opportunity]. |
| Point-of-need support | [Existing or proposed aid], [where to find it], [practice], [maintenance owner]. |
| Evidence | [Measure], [method or source], [eligible group], [collection window and owner], [decision rule or proposed threshold]. |
| Interpretation | [Baseline status], [evidence limits], [other factors], [decision and proposed owner]. |

## Follow-up questions

- Since [event], which describes your experience with [action]? Completed it / Tried and could not complete it / Had an opportunity and did not try / Have not had an opportunity.
- What helped or got in the way? [Offer a few relevant options and an optional short response.]
- If useful, ask about a concrete result such as whether they retrieved the prior decision. Allow an option for people who had no reason to attempt retrieval.

## Stakeholder update

Report the period and group. Include participation, in-class performance, workplace evidence, and operational results only where relevant. For each figure, show its source, count or denominator, and any material limit. For example, if measuring use among respondents with an opportunity, also give the overall response rate and the count with no opportunity. Close with the decision, rationale, and next owner. Use blanks for figures that have yet to be collected.

## Worked example · Optional OneNote for Windows workshop

- **Behavior and outcome.** Organize notes for the next recurring meeting using the practiced structure. When a prior decision is needed, locate it in the notes. The intended outcome is less time spent searching.
- **Transfer.** Ask participants to select a recurring meeting. Propose one optional Teams check-in after that meeting. Let participants who have yet to meet answer after their next opportunity.
- **Support.** Link the existing quick reference card from the workshop resource post and have participants locate it during practice. Use it during work. Follow the workshop's independent-check rules during assessment.
- **Evidence.** Keep observed independent in-class performance distinct from reported use after the workshop. If the pilot includes a timed retrieval task, agree on comparable starting conditions and a correct result. A single prior retrieval attempt can inform the test design. A group baseline requires additional comparable observations.
- **Decision.** Use reported barriers to determine whether to refine the page structure, provide practice, or route an access issue. Collect status and timing without asking participants to submit meeting notes.
