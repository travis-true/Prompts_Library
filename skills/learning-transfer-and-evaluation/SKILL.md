# Learning Transfer and Evaluation

## Anchor the plan in a workplace behavior

- Use the supplied design, task analysis, or Action Mapping handoff. Preserve the approved behavior, assessment rules, audience, and project scope. State the intended business result, then identify the observable workplace action, its trigger, and the standard for acceptable performance. Default to one priority behavior for a small pilot.
- Check for a realistic chance to perform the task and for access, permissions, time, or workflow barriers. Assign proposed owners for barriers within their control. If a missing behavior or constraint would materially change the plan, ask one focused question or supply a clearly labeled provisional plan when feasible.
- Draft within the requested scope. Treat proposed dates, owners, targets, and methods as proposals until confirmed. Keep learner outreach, scheduling, collection, and publication within the user's authorization.

## D4 · Drive learning transfer

- Pair the behavior with an upcoming work event. Specify who will act, when or at what trigger, and who will follow up. Use a low-effort reminder or check-in tied to the event. For optional training, prefer a voluntary check-in unless an existing program specifies another arrangement.
- Give participants a way to report successful use, no opportunity yet, or a barrier. For each likely barrier, identify an appropriate response. Route a permissions issue to the relevant support owner and offer practice when the issue is skill. Include a fallback timing window for infrequent tasks.
- Draft brief follow-up wording when useful. Ask about an action the participant took or attempted and the barrier they encountered. Keep manager involvement proportionate to the program and identify proposed roles when ownership is unsettled.

## D5 · Deploy performance support

- Reuse an existing aid when it fits the task. Specify the decision or steps it supports, where employees can find it during work, how to practice finding and using it, and who keeps it current. Check proposed access and suitability for the learner's device, workflow, and accessibility needs.
- Keep workplace aids available during real work. Follow the authorized assessment rules when an independent check requires unaided performance.

## D6 · Document results

- Match each claim to its evidence. Track reach or completion as participation, confidence as perception, scored practice as learning performance, follow-up answers as reported workplace use, and direct observation or suitable work records as verified workplace behavior. Measure business outcomes with an appropriate operational indicator when feasible. Label the source and limitations of each measure.
- Specify what is measured, how, by whom, and when. Give the eligible population and denominator for each rate, and report response counts. Distinguish no opportunity, missing evidence, unsuccessful attempts, and successful attempts. Use comparable tasks, conditions, and timing if measuring change. Identify absent or weak baselines, selection effects, or other influences before interpreting change. Report causal impact or ROI only when the evidence and evaluation design support it.
- Collect the minimum data needed through approved channels. For IT enablement, favor counts, timings, redacted evidence, or fictional examples. Keep sensitive workplace content out of follow-up submissions unless an approved process explicitly calls for it.
- End the report with a decision tied to the evidence and an owner for any proposed improvement. If no results were supplied, return the evaluation plan or an empty reporting template with placeholders.

## Output

Default to a one-page plan with the workplace behavior and intended outcome, the application opportunity and follow-up, support at the point of need, evidence and collection method, proposed owners and timing, and the decision the evidence will inform. For a narrow request, return the requested component and the context needed to use it. For sample questions, reporting fields, and an IT workshop example, read [templates and examples](references/templates-and-examples.md).
