# Measurement types

| Type | Primary question | Suitable evidence | Common misuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnostic | What does the learner already know or do? | Knowledge items, task sample, interview, artifact | Treating it as a final grade |
| Formative | What feedback or adaptation is needed now? | Checks, practice, observation, explanation | Using low-stakes practice as certification |
| Summative knowledge | Was defined knowledge acquired? | Selected or constructed response | Claiming independent performance |
| Practical or skills | Can the person perform the task under defined conditions? | Work sample, simulation, demonstration | Sampling too little or scoring subjectively |
| Scenario judgment | Can the person make and justify decisions? | Cases, branching scenarios, response rationale | Rewarding test-taking clues rather than judgment |
| Observation | Is the behavior demonstrated in context? | Checklist, rating scale, field observation | Uncalibrated raters or undefined opportunities |
| Confidence/self-efficacy | How ready does the person feel? | Rating and barrier questions | Treating confidence as competence |
| Reaction/experience | How did participants experience the intervention? | Survey, interview, focus group | Claiming learning effectiveness |
| Transfer/behavior | Is the target behavior used at work? | Observation, artifacts, system evidence, structured self-report | Ignoring opportunity, manager, access, or process barriers |
| Operational result | Did a workplace indicator change? | Quality, speed, error, adoption, risk, service data | Attributing change solely to training |
| Implementation/process | Was the intervention delivered and supported as designed? | Fidelity, reach, access, completion, support, defect evidence | Treating delivery fidelity as performance outcome |

## Selection rules

- Use direct evidence when consequences are high.
- Sample enough of the domain to support the intended interpretation.
- Use self-report to measure perception or reported behavior, not verified performance.
- Separate learning evidence from workplace opportunity and environmental support.
- Combine evidence only when the decision rule defines how the pieces interact.
- Do not collect an outcome measure if no owner will act on it.
