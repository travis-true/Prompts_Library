# Feedback, remediation, and reinforcement

## Feedback

Feedback should be:

- Timely
- Specific to criteria
- Focused on the next improvement
- Proportionate to stakes
- Respectful and private when appropriate

## Remediation

For each unmet criterion, define:

- Likely misconception or error
- Corrective explanation or model
- Targeted practice
- Retry conditions
- Recheck evidence
- Escalation or additional support

## Reinforcement

For learners who meet the standard, provide:

- Spaced retrieval
- Advanced scenario
- Transfer task
- Peer explanation
- Job aid
- Follow-up challenge

## Follow-up checkpoints

Connect later evaluation to the original objective and assessment evidence. Avoid follow-up surveys that ask only whether learners remember or enjoyed the course.

## Individual evidence
Record what each learner actually demonstrated, not invented class results. A model or class sample is for teaching; individual proficiency requires the defined individual evidence. State review capacity, per-response scoring time and later feedback when a facilitator cannot score everyone during the session.
