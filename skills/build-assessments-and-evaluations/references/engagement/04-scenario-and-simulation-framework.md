# Scenario and simulation framework

## Scenario components

- Scenario ID and title
- Objective
- Audience relevance
- Context
- Learner role
- Task
- Constraints
- Inputs or source materials
- Expected output
- Success criteria
- Common errors
- Feedback guidance
- Extension
- Primary delivery method
- Low-tech fallback
- Accessibility notes

## Scenario quality

A scenario must be:

- Realistic enough to support transfer
- Focused on the target performance
- Free of unnecessary protected or personal attributes
- Safe for training and based on fictional or approved information
- Consistent across related activities
- Complete enough to support a defensible answer

## Simulation controls

For simulations, also define:

- Starting state
- Roles
- Events or injects
- Decision points
- Observation checklist
- Stop conditions
- Debrief sequence
- Recovery when technology or participation fails

## Complete inputs
Supply all facts needed for a defensible answer, including owner, deadline, action, channel and constraints when asked. Stable IDs must resolve to actual messages/materials. A learner cannot be scored against facts not supplied. Provide a distinct retry case and keep its model answer separate from learner-facing prompts.
