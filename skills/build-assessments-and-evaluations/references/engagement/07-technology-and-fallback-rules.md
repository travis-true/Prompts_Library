# Technology and evidence equivalence

For every technology-dependent activity, record primary task/construct, access/setup, failure signals, fallback task, preserved evidence, omitted evidence, equivalence classification, justification, transition and actual verification status.

## Classification
- **Equivalent:** intended decision/action and evidence requirements are preserved within the stated scope.
- **Partially equivalent:** only some required evidence remains; identify exactly what cannot be concluded.
- **Not equivalent:** the fallback assesses a different construct or only exposes information.

A written response may replace speech when speaking is not the objective. A numbered list may replace drag-and-drop when categorization is the objective. A screenshot sequence, paper simulation or completed-example analysis does not automatically prove actual software operation; it may support recognition/planning only. Do not label it equivalent for an operation objective without actual comparable operation evidence.

Classification is a design judgment; actual testing is separate. An untested or non-equivalent route cannot support omitted proficiency/authorization claims. Preserve access without lowering the required construct: provide an accessible operational environment or reschedule the relevant check; otherwise report the limited evidence and defer the decision. Never invent a tool's accessibility or available permissions.
