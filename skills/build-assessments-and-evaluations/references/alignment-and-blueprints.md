# Alignment and blueprint standards

## Evidence chain

Maintain this chain:

```text
Decision → Outcome → Observable evidence → Method → Item/task → Scoring → Interpretation → Action
```

Every link must be explicit. A break in the chain weakens the interpretation.

## Demand levels

Use task-relevant distinctions:

- recall or recognize;
- explain or interpret;
- apply a rule or procedure;
- analyze evidence or diagnose;
- decide or prioritize;
- create or perform;
- monitor, verify, troubleshoot, or adapt.

Do not use a higher-sounding verb when the item still measures recognition.

## Coverage

- Sample the most important and risky content proportionally.
- Do not overweight trivia because it is easy to test.
- Identify must-pass critical outcomes.
- Avoid redundant items unless needed for reliability or alternate forms.
- Include exceptions and escalation when they are part of competent performance.
- Keep assessment effort proportionate to stakes and available time.

## Parallel forms and pre/post use

When comparing pre and post results:

- keep the construct, coverage, demand, conditions, and scoring comparable;
- avoid identical recall artifacts when exposure would inflate scores;
- document form differences;
- define how missing paired data is handled;
- avoid claiming causation without an appropriate comparison and control of alternatives.

## Traceability

Assign stable IDs:

- `DEC-001` decision
- `OUT-001` outcome
- `INS-001` instrument
- `ITEM-001` item
- `TASK-001` performance task
- `CRIT-001` rubric criterion
- `MET-001` metric
- `RISK-001` risk

Retain source locators and version history for every correct answer and scoring criterion.
