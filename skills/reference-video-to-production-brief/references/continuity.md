## Continuity locks

Record identity traits actually visible, hairstyle, clothing, accessories, props and their states, setting layout, lighting direction, color treatment, screen direction, eye line, scale, and each shot's entry/exit pose. Preserve these across shot prompts unless a planned change is explicit.

For replacement, specify retain/change/unknown separately. Do not infer sensitive personal attributes or invent unseen identity details. If a visible identity mismatch or uncertain likeness use materially affects the task, resolve it before generation. Treat preserving identity as a goal to evaluate, not a guarantee.

Check hands/object contact, facial consistency during turns, wardrobe drift, prop count, and background changes. For multiple characters, use stable labels and map each reference independently. Avoid contradictory reference priorities; ask when two supplied sources conflict on a required feature.
