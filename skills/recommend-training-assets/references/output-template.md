# Recommendation brief template

Use only sections that add decision value.

## Recommended package

State the smallest effective package in one to three sentences.

| Priority | Asset | Action | Purpose | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Required / Recommended / Optional | Asset name | Reuse / Revise / Create / Retire | Job the asset performs | Evidence-based rationale |

## Delivery and reinforcement

Describe how the assets work together before, during, and after performance. Identify delivery mode, sequence, access, support, and reinforcement only when relevant.

## Measurement

State:

- the decision the evidence must support;
- the minimum measure;
- the owner or reviewer, if known;
- why additional assessment is unnecessary, if omitted.

## Assets already available

| Existing asset | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name or description | Reuse / Revise / Replace / Retire / Not assessed | Suitability and gap |

## Assumptions, gaps, and risks

Label each item:

- `FACT`
- `ASSUMPTION`
- `INFERENCE`
- `NOT PROVIDED`
- `NOT VERIFIED`
- `DECISION REQUIRED`

State the effect each unresolved item could have on the recommendation.

## Alternatives

Provide at most two:

1. **Lean alternative:** What it removes, saves, and risks.
2. **Expanded alternative:** What it adds and what condition justifies it.

Omit alternatives when there is only one defensible route.

## Confidence and approval gate

- **Confidence:** High / Medium / Low
- **Basis:** Evidence and limitations
- **Approval requested:** Specific package or decision
- **Next step after approval:** Production plan, source collection, prototype, pilot, or another defined action

End after the approval gate unless production was explicitly authorized.
