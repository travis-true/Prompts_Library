# Review quality check

Record every applicable check separately with applicability/rationale, status (Pass / Needs revision / Not checked), method, actual evidence location/result, reviewer, date, reviewed artifact/version and corrective action. Excluded checks have Not applicable, rationale and no Pass.

| ID | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Q-01 | Each defect has original evidence or a documented gap |
| Q-02 | Severity matches impact and scope |
| Q-03 | Strengths and required scope are preserved or explicitly treated |
| Q-04 | Original and proposed objective alignment are distinguished |
| Q-05 | Time was calculated or honestly marked Not checked |
| Q-06 | Activities and assessments were reviewed at claimed maturity |
| Q-07 | Accessibility-design, rendered materials and delivery scope are separated |
| Q-08 | Sources and applicable higher-risk controls were evaluated |
| Q-09 | Structural revisions have explicit rationale |
| Q-10 | Every material change maps to a finding or requirement |
| Q-11 | Version-specific dispositions and closure follow the gate |
| Q-12 | No unsupported approval, readiness, execution or closure claim exists |

These checks assess the review artifact. They do not establish that the underlying course, future materials or delivery passed their own checks.
