# Activity, assessment, and facilitation review

## Learner activity review

For each activity, determine:

- Objective supported
- Learner action
- Time
- Output or evidence
- Feedback method
- Debrief
- Accessibility alternative
- Delivery-format fit
- Fallback

Participation alone is not evidence of learning. A poll, discussion, or whiteboard activity must serve a defined purpose.

## Demonstration review

A useful demonstration normally includes:

- Goal and scenario
- Starting state
- Visible procedure or reasoning
- Common failure point
- Recovery
- Expected result
- Takeaway
- Immediate practice

## Assessment review

Determine whether the assessment measures the objective:

- Recognition objectives: identification, categorization, or short response
- Decision objectives: scenario choice with rationale
- Procedure objectives: observed completion or checklist
- Communication objectives: produced artifact evaluated against criteria
- Troubleshooting objectives: diagnosis and correction
- Judgment objectives: case evaluation using standards

## Success criteria

Criteria must be clear enough that a facilitator or learner can determine whether the output meets the goal.

## Facilitator usability

A facilitator-ready outline should provide, as appropriate:

- Purpose
- Timing
- Facilitator action
- Learner action
- Materials
- Exact prompts
- Expected evidence
- Debrief
- Transition
- Fallback

Flag vague instructions such as “discuss,” “demo,” or “practice” when the outline does not say what to do, observe, or produce.

## Review versus development
Check whether supplied or claimed facilitator-ready practice includes all facts, files, instructions, model answers, acceptable alternatives, critical criteria, total threshold, feedback and equivalent retry. An outline may reasonably defer production details; label maturity and developer acceptance criteria rather than claiming these assets exist. All critical criteria must pass regardless of total score when performance depends on them. Class sampling or self-ratings do not verify every learner. Route unfinished production to Live Training Session Design with stable IDs.
