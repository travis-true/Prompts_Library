# Higher-risk and software controls

## Higher-risk outlines

For compliance, legal, safety, health, privacy, financial, security, or regulated topics:

- Identify controlling sources.
- Preserve exact requirements and defined terms when meaning could change.
- Flag unsupported, altered, or simplified requirements.
- Separate instructional-design review from subject-matter validation.
- Require qualified subject-matter review when evidence is absent.
- Prevent a favorable scoped disposition when required qualified review is not documented.
- Do not claim compliance or formal approval.

## Software outlines

Review and classify:

- Product and application
- Web, desktop, mobile, or mixed platform
- Version, edition, browser, or operating system when material
- License and entitlement
- Tenant or administrator settings
- Required accounts and permissions
- Integration and dependency assumptions
- Practice-data safety
- Demo setup and fallback

Classify features as:

- Verified available
- Environment-dependent
- License-dependent
- Preview
- Announced or roadmap
- Retired or deprecated
- Not verified

Required hands-on practice should not depend on unverified, inaccessible, preview, or retired capabilities.

## Gate mapping
Apply the current labels from references/08-disposition-gate.md; the older Approved as-is wording denotes only its legacy equivalent. Missing mandatory qualified review blocks a favorable determination in its applicable scope. Separate general product evidence from actual tenant/access checks; unperformed environment tests remain Not checked.
