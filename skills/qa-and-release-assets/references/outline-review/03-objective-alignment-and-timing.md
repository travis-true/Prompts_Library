# Objective alignment and timing review

## Objective review

Check whether objectives are:

- Relevant to the stated need
- Observable
- Appropriate for the audience
- Achievable within the session
- Practiced
- Assessed
- Consistent with LMS or course-description promises

Avoid treating understand, know, learn, or appreciate as measurable unless the outline defines observable evidence.

## Alignment matrix

Build this matrix:

| Objective | Instruction or demonstration | Practice | Assessment or evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Status values:

- Aligned
- Partially aligned
- Not aligned
- Not reviewable

## Timing calculation

Record:

- Scheduled start and end
- Total elapsed time
- Break time
- Fixed administrative time
- Usable instructional time
- Sum of agenda rows
- Variance

## Timing findings

Review whether:

- Agenda minutes equal available time
- Breaks are counted correctly
- Opening, transitions, questions, and close are represented
- Demonstrations include setup and recovery time
- Practice time matches task complexity
- Assessments include response and feedback time
- Optional topics are distinguishable
- The agenda protects the stated objectives

## Overload priority

When reducing time, recommend this order:

1. Remove duplication
2. Defer optional or advanced content
3. Reduce the number of examples
4. Simplify supporting explanation
5. Narrow objectives or extend the session

Do not recommend removing required practice or assessment merely to make the arithmetic fit.

## Time accounting and objective preservation
Classify supplied rows as instruction, break or fixed administration when evidence supports that classification. Unknown allocation stays unknown, not zero. Opening and closing can be instruction when they serve learning; classify once. A proposed agenda may explicitly assume zero separate administration if it labels that assumption. Session total equals the sum of all rows; instructional time excludes breaks and fixed administration. Distinguish source arithmetic from proposed arithmetic.

Map every original objective ID to preserved/revised/deferred/removed treatment, revised objective ID(s), rationale, finding/source IDs, decision owner and decision status. Do not silently drop tone or other original scope when substituting measurable objectives. Any proposed narrowing needs owner confirmation; keep the dependency open.
