# Original fictional outline: Better workplace messages

**Duration:** 90 minutes  
**Audience:** Employees  
**Format:** Live workshop

## Objectives

- Understand good communication.
- Learn how to write better messages.
- Know how tone affects communication.

## Agenda

| Minutes | Topic |
|---:|---|
| 10 | Welcome and introductions |
| 25 | Communication basics lecture |
| 20 | Tone lecture |
| 20 | Email best practices lecture |
| 20 | Examples and discussion |
| 10 | Practice |
| 10 | Questions and wrap-up |

## Topics

### Communication basics

- Be clear.
- Be concise.
- Be professional.
- Use the right tone.

### Tone

Discuss formal, informal, positive, and negative tone.

### Email best practices

- Use a good subject line.
- Include enough detail.
- Proofread.

### Examples and discussion

Show several good and bad emails. Ask learners what they notice.

### Practice

Learners rewrite an email.

### Questions and wrap-up

Answer questions and summarize the session.
