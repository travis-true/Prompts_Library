# Objectives and alignment

## Objective pattern

Write objectives as:

`Given [conditions], the learner will [observable action] to [criterion or standard].`

Conditions and criteria may be omitted when they add no value, but objectives must still describe observable performance.

## Preferred verbs

Use verbs such as:

- Identify
- Select
- Explain
- Organize
- Demonstrate
- Complete
- Apply
- Compare
- Troubleshoot
- Revise
- Evaluate against criteria

Avoid relying on unobservable verbs such as understand, know, learn, or appreciate.

## Objective quality check

Each objective must be:

- Relevant to the learner need
- Observable
- Teachable within the session
- Practiced
- Assessed or checked
- Written at an appropriate level

## Alignment matrix

Every course must include an alignment matrix:

| Objective | Instruction or demonstration | Practice | Assessment or success evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

An objective fails alignment when any of the final three columns is empty.

## Assessment selection

Match the evidence to the objective:

- Recall or recognition: poll, short response, categorization
- Decision making: scenario choice with rationale
- Procedure: observed task completion or checklist
- Communication: produced message evaluated against criteria
- Troubleshooting: diagnosis and corrective action
- Judgment: case analysis using defined standards

## LMS learning objectives

LMS objectives must match the live course. Do not publish broader promises than the session teaches or assesses.

## Grounded scoring
Supply all practice facts before asking learners to act. Use stable objective/activity/assessment/criterion IDs. Include a facilitator-only model answer with annotations and acceptable alternatives. Make critical criteria mandatory regardless of total score. State the numeric threshold, feedback, remediation and equivalent retry. Do not infer individual success from a sample of class responses. For informal learning, label unobserved individual results as unknown; if completion requires demonstrated performance, collect and score each required response.
