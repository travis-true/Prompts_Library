# Agenda and timing rules

## Time calculation

Calculate:

`Usable instructional time = total session time - breaks - fixed administrative time`

Then reserve time for:

- Opening and orientation
- Transitions
- Questions and recovery
- Final assessment or success check
- Close and next steps

## Adaptive pacing

Use topic complexity and learner action to set timing. Do not apply one fixed ratio to every course.

Typical planning ranges are suggestions, not mandatory standards:

- Opening and orientation: about 5% to 10%
- Closing and transfer: about 5% to 10%
- Learner action, practice, feedback, and assessment: aim for at least 35% when the course promises skill performance
- Contingency: protect a small buffer when live technology or group discussion is involved

## Breaks

- Honor user-supplied break requirements.
- Place breaks near logical transitions.
- Recalculate usable time after adding a break.
- Do not treat break time as instructional time.

## Agenda validity

Required checks:

- Row minutes sum to the available schedule.
- Breaks are included accurately.
- Start and end times are consistent.
- No module has more content than its time permits.
- Practice and assessment time are visible, not implied.
- Transitions and setup are accounted for when material.

## Overload response

If the plan exceeds the available time:

1. Preserve objectives, practice, and assessment.
2. Remove or defer optional and supporting content.
3. Reduce the number of examples before reducing practice quality.
4. Convert advanced material to follow-up resources.
5. Recommend a longer session or narrower scope when necessary.
6. Mark timing as `Needs revision` until balanced.

## Consistent accounting
Classify each row as instruction, break, or administration. Total row minutes must equal total session minutes. Break and administration sums must match their declared totals. Instruction sum must equal total minus breaks minus fixed administration. Opening, Q&A and closing may count as instructional time when they support learning; classify once and do not subtract them again. Use elapsed minutes unless a start time, date and timezone are supplied. Substeps must fit their parent row; show core and optional variants separately rather than double-counting them.
