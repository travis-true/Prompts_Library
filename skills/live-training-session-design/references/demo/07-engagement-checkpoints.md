# Engagement checkpoint guidance

## Before the demo
Use relevance, prediction, or goal-setting prompts.

## During the demo
Use observation prompts, pause-and-predict, decision points, error spotting, or brief rationale checks.

## After the demo
Use debrief, retrieval, transfer, or practice handoff.

## Required fields
- Placement
- Objective
- Exact prompt
- Response mode
- Expected evidence
- Facilitator response
- Maximum time
- Low-tech fallback

Avoid interaction where it interrupts a fragile sequence, exposes private screens, or distracts from a critical action.
