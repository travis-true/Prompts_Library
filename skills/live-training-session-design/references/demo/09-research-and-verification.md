# Research and verification rules

## Research when needed
Verify current software interfaces, licensing, availability, permissions, product limitations, regulated requirements, or changing workflows when they affect demo accuracy.

## Source priority
1. User-supplied authoritative source
2. Official product, standard, regulator, or process owner source
3. Reputable secondary source for labeled recommendations
4. Clearly labeled professional judgment

## Verification record
Record:
- Claim or step
- Source
- Version or date when available
- Verification status
- Environment dependency
- Review needed

Do not infer current availability from an announced or preview feature. Label unverified behavior and add a pre-demo validation step.


Verify actual environment behavior separately from documentation. Include source ID/version/locator, claim ID, environment dependency, actual method/date/reviewer and status. Fictional product claims are Not applicable to current-product research, not a verified real application.
