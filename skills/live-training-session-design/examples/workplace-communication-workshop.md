## LMS-ready listing

### Title
Write clear workplace messages

### Description
Clear workplace messages help people understand what matters and act without unnecessary follow-up. In this 90-minute live workshop, learners will examine common message problems, watch a message revision, practice improving two examples, and complete an independent communication challenge.

The course focuses on practical choices: lead with the purpose, include only useful context, make the request specific, and state ownership and timing when they matter.

### Audience
Employees who write internal email, chat, project updates, or process reminders.

### Prerequisites
No specialist prerequisites; learners need a way to read and write a short message.

### Duration and delivery format
90 minutes, delivered virtually or in person.

### Completion requirements
Participate in practice and submit the final message, criterion self-rating and one explanation. Attendance or submission alone does not establish mastery. Confirm any sponsor-specific requirements before publishing the LMS listing.

### Learning objectives

By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

- Identify unclear purpose, missing context, vague action, and absent timing in a workplace message.
- Revise a workplace message using a purpose-context-action structure.
- Evaluate a revised message against five success criteria and explain one improvement.

## Assumptions and constraints

- Learners can type or write a short response.
- A shared document, chat, paper worksheet, or editable file is available.
- Examples use fictional projects and people.
- This course teaches clarity, not organization-specific writing policy.

## Module guides

### Module 1: Welcome and relevance

**Purpose:** Establish why clarity matters and surface existing learner judgment.  
**Time:** 8 minutes  
**Objectives supported:** Preparation for all objectives.

#### Facilitator actions

Display these messages:

**Message A:** “Following up on the update. There are several things we should discuss before moving ahead. Let me know.”

**Message B:** “Please review the draft schedule by 3 p.m. Thursday and add comments where the timing creates a conflict. I will publish the revised schedule Friday morning.”

Ask: “Which message would be easier to act on, and what makes the difference?”

#### Learner action

Vote and provide one reason.

#### Evidence

Learners mention purpose, action, context, ownership, or timing.

#### Fallback

If discussion is slow, ask learners to underline the exact action in each message.

### Module 2: Diagnose unclear messages

**Purpose:** Teach a reusable diagnostic scan.  
**Time:** 12 minutes  
**Objective:** Identify unclear purpose, missing context, vague action, and absent timing.

#### Facilitator actions

Introduce four questions:

- Why is the reader receiving this?
- What context is necessary?
- What must the reader do?
- Who owns the next step, and when is it needed?

Use this fictional message:

> We talked about the onboarding guide and there are a few updates. Some sections may need work because things have changed. Can everyone take a look and let me know what you think?

#### Exact check-for-understanding prompt

“Identify one phrase that creates uncertainty and name the information the reader still needs.”

#### Expected evidence

Examples include no deadline, no section ownership, no definition of “take a look,” and unclear changes.

### Module 3: Demonstration

**Purpose:** Model purpose-context-action revision.  
**Time:** 15 minutes

#### Demonstration

- **Setup:** A project coordinator needs three reviewers to update a fictional onboarding guide.
- **Starting state:** Use the unclear message from Module 2.
- **Process:** Lead with the purpose, state what changed, assign the review action, and add timing and ownership.
- **Failure point:** Adding every detail makes the message longer without making the action clearer.
- **Recovery:** Remove background that does not change what the reader must do.
- **Finished example:**

> Please review your assigned section of the onboarding guide by noon Wednesday. Update any steps that changed after the new request process began, then mark your section complete in the review list. I will combine the updates Wednesday afternoon.

- **Takeaway:** Clarity comes from making the reader’s next action visible.

#### Learner prediction prompt

“Before I revise the next sentence, what information should I add or remove?”

### Module 4: Guided practice

**Purpose:** Apply the structure with support.  
**Time:** 15 minutes

#### Scenario

A fictional project lead needs team members to report testing problems before a Friday launch.

#### Fictional scenario facts (S-02, guided task)

- Launch: Friday; no calendar date is required for this exercise.
- Report testing problems by noon Thursday.
- Enter each problem in the fictional Test Issues shared list.
- Include the affected task, observed result, expected result and impact.
- Morgan Chen, the project lead, reviews submissions Thursday afternoon.
- Do not promise a fix or introduce another deadline.

#### Starting message

> We are getting close to launch. If there are issues, we should probably know about them soon so they can be addressed. Please send anything you find.

#### Instructions

1. Put the purpose in the first sentence.
2. Add only the context needed to act.
3. State what to report and where.
4. Add the deadline and identify the next owner.
5. Check the message against the five criteria.

#### Success criteria

The revised message includes a clear purpose, definition of what to report, submission method, deadline, and next owner.

#### Try more

Reduce the message to three sentences without losing any required information.

### Module 5: Feedback and debrief

**Time:** 8 minutes

Ask learners to mark each criterion as present or missing. Then ask:

“What did you remove because it did not help the reader act?”

If peer review is not accessible or practical, learners complete a self-review using the same criteria.

### Module 6: Final performance challenge

**Time:** 17 minutes

#### Task

Revise this message independently:

> Reminder that the workspace move is happening and people have had questions. There is a list that needs to be checked. Please look at it and respond if something is wrong.

#### Conditions

The fictional move list must be confirmed by 2 p.m. Tuesday. Employees should verify their work location and equipment needs in the shared list. Jordan Lee will consolidate corrections.

#### Success criteria

The message meets all critical criteria C1, C3, C4 and C5 AND earns at least four of five points. Any invented requirement fails the task regardless of score. Assessment A-04 separately requires accurate self-ratings and one grounded explanation of an improvement. See the facilitator-only key.

#### Evidence

- Revised message
- Self-rating against all five criteria
- One sentence explaining the most important revision

#### Feedback

Facilitator samples responses for whole-group feedback and corrects one common gap without identifying learners. Sampling does not establish every learner’s success. Collect individual responses if completion requires demonstrated performance; score those after the session using the key. This example proposes participation plus submission for completion, with individual mastery unverified until scored.

#### Support

Learners who need more support receive the purpose-context-action sentence starters, targeted feedback on the missing criterion, and an equivalent retry using the supplied retry scenario. Do not report an individual Pass without reviewing that individual’s evidence.

### Module 7: Review and transfer

**Time:** 10 minutes

Ask:

“What is one message you routinely send that would improve if the action appeared earlier?”

Close with this transfer check:

- Before sending, find the purpose.
- Find the reader’s action.
- Confirm ownership and timing.
- Remove context that does not help the reader act.

## Facilitator-only assessment key

Keep this section separate from learner handouts until the corresponding assessment ends.

### A-01: Diagnose (O-01)
Expected issues: vague purpose, unspecified changes, undefined review action, missing section ownership, missing deadline. Pass the formative check when at least three supported issues are named and one is explained. Accept equivalent explanations. If fewer appear, revisit the four scan questions and retry with the testing message.

### A-02: Guided rewrite (O-02)
Model answer: “Please report testing problems in the Test Issues shared list by noon Thursday so the project lead can review them before Friday’s launch. For each problem, include the affected task, observed result, expected result and impact. Morgan Chen will review submissions Thursday afternoon.”

This model uses only supplied scenario facts: purpose (prepare launch review), necessary context (Friday launch), action (record defined problem details), owner (Morgan), timing (noon Thursday / Thursday afternoon). Other concise wording is acceptable. Do not invent a resolution promise, tool, date or responsible person. For this supported task, require purpose, useful context and specific action; provide feedback on missing ownership/timing before independent practice.

### A-03: Independent rewrite (O-02)

| Criterion | Points | Critical? | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 Purpose | 1 | Yes | Early request to confirm the move list |
| C2 Useful context | 1 | No | Brief explanation that the workspace move is approaching |
| C3 Specific action | 1 | Yes | Verify work location and equipment needs in the shared list and identify corrections |
| C4 Owner | 1 | Yes | Jordan Lee consolidates corrections |
| C5 Timing | 1 | Yes | Confirmation by 2 p.m. Tuesday |

**Pass:** all critical criteria AND at least 4/5 points; any invented requirement overrides the score and fails the task. Optional context cannot compensate for a missing action, owner or deadline.

**Model answer:** “Please confirm your work location and equipment needs in the shared move list by 2 p.m. Tuesday. Identify any corrections in the list; Jordan Lee will consolidate them for the workspace move.”

**Annotations:** Sentence 1 supplies C1, C3 and C5. Sentence 2 supplies C4 and useful move context C2. Accept equivalent wording such as a concise title stating the purpose, provided the actual required action and deadline remain explicit. Never invent a link or calendar date.

### A-04: Evaluate and explain (O-03)
Require both E1 (each of five self-ratings agrees with the submitted message) and E2 (one explanation connects an actual revision to clearer reader action). Each earns one point; both are critical and 2/2 is required. The facilitator must inspect the message and self-rating before recording a result; independent self-report alone does not verify accuracy. A valid explanation might say “I replaced ‘look at it’ with the two fields employees must check, so the review action is specific.”
