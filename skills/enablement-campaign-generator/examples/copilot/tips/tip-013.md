**Version:** 1.1.0
**Phase:** Daily habits · **Difficulty:** Foundational · **Mode:** application

## Scenario and complete fictional inputs

Notes: October 21, Morgan approved a two-week pilot. Jordan suggested a November launch, but no launch decision was made. Casey will draft pilot instructions.
