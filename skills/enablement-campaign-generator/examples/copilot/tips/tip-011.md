# TIP-011: Plan from supplied priorities

**Version:** 1.1.0
**Phase:** Daily habits · **Difficulty:** Foundational · **Mode:** repeat-use
**State:** Draft; no release, schedule or publication authorization.

## Scenario and complete fictional inputs
Calendar urgency crowds out blocked work.

Monday plan: a report due Tuesday needs 60 minutes and has all inputs. A guide due Friday needs 90 minutes but awaits approval. Available focus time today: 90 minutes.

## Illustrative model result
Spend 60 minutes on the Tuesday report; send an approval-status request for the guide; reserve remaining time for review/buffer. Do not count the blocked 90-minute guide as completed.
