# Measurement framework

## Interpret signals narrowly
Exposure is reach; reactions/clicks are engagement; submitting a prompt or reporting an attempt is attempted activity. Learning requires outcome-aligned evidence and defined scoring/interpretation. Confidence and self-reported repeat use are perception/report measures, not independent performance proof. Work samples can support bounded application observations after an authorized review. No causal or productivity claim follows automatically.

## Definition per selected metric
Give stable metric ID, decision, layer, numerator, denominator or explicit not-applicable reason, eligible population, period, de-duplication, missing-data handling, actual source/access, collection and analysis owner, baseline/target status, analysis method, interpretation limit and intended action. Store unknown baseline/target/results as null. Proposed targets need rationale and owner approval; do not invent benchmarks or label hypothetical results observed. Voluntary self-selection and nonresponse limit population claims.

## Data governance
Declare collection status separately from metric design. Before collecting employee-level telemetry or work samples, obtain the actual permitted-use/privacy/access/retention plan; do not infer permission from campaign ownership. Use minimal aggregate or voluntary sanitized evidence when appropriate and approved. Define access/retention/deletion owner, aggregation/small-group disclosure controls and support for withdrawal where applicable. Do not require public posting of work content to participate.

Use [the measurement template](../../templates/copilot/measurement-dashboard.md). Hand off consequential learning/qualification interpretations to Build Assessments and Evaluations, retaining IDs and sources.
