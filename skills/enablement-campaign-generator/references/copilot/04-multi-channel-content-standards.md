# Multi-channel standards

## Teams
Short hook, practical value, prompt, action, review reminder, support path. Optimize for scan and conversation.

## SharePoint
Durable explanation with what it is, why it matters, steps, example prompt, cautions, next action, resources, and alt text.

## Email
Campaign-level launch, periodic rollup, leader reinforcement, and close. Do not send one full email per daily tip unless explicitly required.

## Prompt card
Scenario, fill-in prompt, expected output, review checklist, variations, and source status.

## Leader talking points
Why this matters, expected behavior, one example, responsible-use boundary, reinforcement ask, and help path.

Channel adaptations must preserve the target behavior but may vary in tone, length, and depth.


Maintain one canonical tip ID and separate channel asset IDs/version/state. Use text-specific channel adaptations; do not claim native rendering or publication from Markdown drafts.
