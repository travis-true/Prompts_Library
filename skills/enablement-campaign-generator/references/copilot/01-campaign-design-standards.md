# Campaign design standards

A campaign must define: objective, audience, behavior target, journey phase, cadence, channels, content spine, review route, publishing owner, support path, measurement, and expiration or refresh rule.

Classify content as core, reinforcement, optional, advanced, seasonal, licensed, environment-dependent, or verify before release.

Avoid content saturation. Reuse one idea across channels with different depth: short action in Teams, durable explanation in SharePoint, summary and call to action in email, reusable prompt on a card.

Every asset needs one clear next step. Avoid vague calls to action such as “learn more” when a task, prompt, practice, registration, or support route is available.
