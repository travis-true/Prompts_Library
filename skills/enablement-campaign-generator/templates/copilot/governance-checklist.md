# Campaign governance checklist

- [ ] Product claims verified
- [ ] Licensing and environment dependencies labeled
- [ ] Responsible-use and policy review complete
- [ ] Human-review reminder included
- [ ] Brand or communications review complete when required
- [ ] Accessibility reviewed
- [ ] Publishing owner identified for every channel
- [ ] Support route verified
- [ ] Metrics have owners, sources, limits, and privacy rules
- [ ] Expiration or refresh date assigned
- [ ] Final release decision documented


## Traceability and actual evidence
Record artifact/source/claim/tip/channel-asset/metric IDs, versions, owner, expiry and review/approval/publication status separately. Supply complete task-specific inputs, expected model and human check; alt text is provisional until rendered. Calendar: sequence-only or confirmed dates/cadence/timezone; a plan is not scheduled. Preserve unknowns and block unsupported release.
