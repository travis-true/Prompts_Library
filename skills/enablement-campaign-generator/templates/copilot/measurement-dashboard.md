# Selected metric specification

ID; layer; decision/action; numerator; denominator or reason not applicable; eligible population; period; deduplication/missingness; source/access status; collection/analysis owner; baseline/target/results (null when unknown); target authority; analysis; limits; privacy/access/retention/deletion; collection authorization status and evidence. Attempt metrics cannot be labeled demonstrated learning. Self-reported use/time remains self-report. No causal or proficiency claim without appropriate design/evidence.
