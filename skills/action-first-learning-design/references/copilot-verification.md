# Verify a Copilot-drafted follow-up

**Job action** — Verify action-item owners and deadlines against meeting notes before sending a follow-up.

**Setup** — Seven minutes in Teams. Provide the two fictional cases as readable text in sequence. Learners can work in their workbook and submit the retry through a private channel the facilitator can review. Use prepared drafts and notes, so learners can practice without access to Copilot.

## Learner materials

**Opening case**

- Meeting notes: “Rina will circulate the updated onboarding checklist. Noah will collect team feedback. The team will agree on a checklist due date at the next check-in.”
- Draft: “Rina will circulate the updated onboarding checklist. Malik will collect team feedback. The checklist is due Friday.”
- Prompt: Mark the claims you would change before sending. Point to what the notes actually support.
- Optional opening cue: Check each action item for its owner and any confirmed deadline.

**Independent retry**

- Meeting notes: “Priya will circulate the revised meeting-room guide. Elena will collect sign-off. The team will set the distribution date after the accessibility review.”
- Draft: “Priya will circulate the revised meeting-room guide. Omar will collect sign-off. The guide will go out on June 12.”
- Prompt: Identify two unsupported details, cite the relevant wording from the notes, and rewrite the follow-up so it is ready to send.

## Facilitator sequence and key

1. **Opening attempt, 2 minutes.** Collect individual responses. Rina's assignment is supported. The notes name Noah as the owner for collecting feedback. Friday is unconfirmed because the team will agree on a date at the next check-in.
2. **Feedback and demonstration, 2 minutes.** Trace each action and deadline to the notes. Show how to retain Rina's confirmed assignment, replace Malik with Noah, and state that the due date will be agreed at the next check-in. Explain that unsupported assignments can send work to the wrong person and unconfirmed dates can create commitments.
3. **Independent retry, 2 minutes.** Release the fresh case. Allow the notes and draft as work materials. Withhold hints and answer reveals until submission. Success requires flagging Omar as the wrong owner and June 12 as unconfirmed, citing the relevant source evidence, and retaining Priya's confirmed assignment while naming Elena for sign-off and leaving distribution timing pending until after the accessibility review.
4. **Reflection, 1 minute.** Ask, “What will you verify before sending your next AI-drafted follow-up?” Reveal the retry key after submissions.
