## Additional nonofficial cross-checks (not official authority)

## Maintenance protocol
1. For release work, inspect the **official help center or upload dialog** for the precise placement; record URL, date and requirements.
2. Update `platform-dimensions.md` if a discrepancy is confirmed; record source and review date next to that row.
3. Keep sizes labeled **graphic reference / CHECK** where not independently verified; do not silently present them as platform policy.
4. Review high-change short-form and profile/banner UI placements at least quarterly if used in an ongoing production workflow.
5. Consider changing a recommendation without deleting the historical reference; note why it was superseded.
