# Presentation QA and release

## Content QA

- Every required topic is included or explicitly excluded.
- Claims are accurate, scoped, and traceable.
- Terminology, dates, names, units, and labels are consistent.
- Visible copy is audience-facing.
- Notes contain delivery detail without contradicting the slide.
- Placeholders, draft markers, and production comments are removed.

## Narrative QA

- Communication job and audience outcome are clear.
- Opening creates relevance or a question worth resolving.
- Each slide has one main job and advances the arc.
- Evidence is interpreted.
- Transitions prevent logical jumps.
- Close resolves the opening and defines the appropriate outcome.

## Visual QA

- Hierarchy, alignment, spacing, contrast, and balance are consistent.
- Adjacent slides do not repeat the same composition mechanically.
- Titles do not wrap unexpectedly.
- Text is readable without excessive density.
- Images are sharp, correctly cropped, and relevant.
- Charts, tables, diagrams, screenshots, and callouts are legible and accurate.
- No unintended overlap, clipping, off-canvas content, or broken connectors remains.

## Template-fidelity QA

- Correct master and layouts are used.
- Inherited elements remain inherited where possible.
- Fonts, palette, margins, recurring elements, and footer behavior match.
- Template placeholders are used correctly.
- Unauthorized template redesign or mixing did not occur.

## Accessibility QA

- Unique titles and logical reading order are present.
- Contrast, type size, alt text, tables, media alternatives, and equivalent interactions are verified where supported.
- Color, audio, animation, or position is not the sole carrier of meaning.
- Status is `NOT VERIFIED` for checks the host cannot perform.

## Technical QA

- Requested format, dimensions, editability, links, notes, media, and metadata are correct.
- Every slide renders successfully.
- Final slide order and count match the approved plan.
- Charts and data survive export.
- File opens without repair warnings.
- Final version is distinct from working files.

## Source QA

- Non-trivial external claims and assets have traceable source notes.
- Rights or permitted-use restrictions are respected.
- No invented citations or unsupported evidence appears.
- Source limitations are disclosed where material.

## Release statuses

- `READY`: All required checks passed and approvals are evidenced.
- `READY WITH CONDITIONS`: Defined non-blocking conditions remain.
- `NOT READY`: A blocker prevents the intended use.
- `NOT VERIFIED`: Required checks were not performed or evidence is unavailable.

Report what was inspected. Never infer a pass from successful file creation alone.
