# Performance barrier diagnostic — original decision aid

Apply **per priority job action**; there may be multiple barriers.

1. **Evidence check:** Is the difficulty observed, reported, hypothesized, or unknown? Have representative employees/SMEs been asked or observed? If not, record an evidence gap.
2. **Environment/access:** Can a capable worker do the task with current permissions, reliable tools, information, time, and working conditions? If not, consider access/tool/workflow action; training alone is insufficient.
3. **Process/expectations:** Is there a clear process, usable standard, ownership, and expected outcome? If not, clarify/revise expectations and workflow.
4. **Feedback/motivation:** Are the consequences, incentives, manager feedback, and prioritization aligned? If not, investigate managerial/system changes rather than assuming an attitude problem.
5. **Knowledge:** Do employees lack factual/procedural information that can be supplied at the moment of need? If yes, consider a short explanation, search guidance, or a job aid.
6. **Practice/decision-making:** Can employees explain the steps yet struggle with applying them or choosing correctly in context? If yes, realistic practice, examples, coaching, and feedback may help.
7. **Validate alternatives:** Do expert performers use a different route? Could a checklist, default setting, process change, or template eliminate the need to memorize?
8. **Mixed barriers:** Capture multiple interventions where justified; do not declare a false single root cause.
