# Illustrative example — meeting-note retrieval (NOT an approved project change)

**Purpose:** Demonstrate safe use of the skill on an example resembling a corporate OneNote training need. This is only a **proposal**, not a tested activity, verified diagnostic, or new approval.

## Discovery snapshot

**Reported workplace problem:** People take meeting notes in several locations/formats and can spend time locating prior decisions.

**Single observation:** One retrieval attempt involved opening six files and took 6 minutes 40 seconds. Treat it as one example, **not** a group average or baseline.

**Outcome candidate:** Improve the ability to locate the correct meeting decision and its source. A future evaluation might examine correctness, completion, and retrieval time; target and baseline remain **UNKNOWN**.
