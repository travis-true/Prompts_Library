# Evidence-based review rubric

Do not invent point totals. Mark each applicable dimension:

- **Supported:** clear evidence in authorized sources.
- **Partial:** some evidence but a meaningful gap.
- **Missing:** materials inspected and the element is absent.
- **Cannot determine:** evidence or materials are insufficient.
- **Not applicable:** a defensible reason the element is not relevant.

For each finding include: `criterion | status | source/location | rationale | proposed change | approval/validation dependency`.

## Dimensions

1. **Job outcome:** performance goal stated in employee/business terms, with measure or explicitly proposed metric.
2. **Task traceability:** goals connected to observable real-world employee actions.
3. **Barrier analysis:** different causes tested rather than treating every problem as a knowledge gap.
4. **Intervention fit:** training versus non-training interventions justified by evidence.
5. **Authentic practice:** decisions/tasks match job constraints; error paths are credible.
6. **Feedback:** consequence-based feedback leads to better future decisions.
7. **Minimal information:** only necessary content, preferably accessible during tasks if available at work.
8. **Performance assessment:** independent evidence of actions; supported practice distinguished from independent use.
9. **Transfer/evaluation:** after-training performance signals separated from course completion/satisfaction/confidence.
10. **Accessibility/technical feasibility:** learner access, assistive technology, prerequisite platforms, permissions, safe test environments.
11. **Source and approval integrity:** cited evidence, exact source versions, approved versus proposed decisions, active holds.

## Issue priority (no fabricated scoring)

- **Critical:** creates unsafe/unapproved action, contradicts approved design, or misrepresents evidence/validation.
- **High:** likely misses a central workplace action or falsely assumes training fixes a non-training barrier.
- **Medium:** useful practice or measurement improvement that is not essential to current approval.
- **Low:** optional polish that does not affect learning-performance alignment.

Report up to five high-value improvements first. A content-review audit does not authorize edits.
