# Methodology, provenance, and attribution

## Design basis

This is an **original operational skill** informed by performance consulting, backward design, job-task analysis, and Cathy Moore's publicly described action-mapping approach; it does **not** copy text, diagrams, worksheets, examples, or other proprietary contents from *Map It: The Hands-on Guide to Strategic Training Design* (2017). Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a substitute for Cathy Moore's materials.

The public process emphasizes: identify a business goal; identify observable work actions; investigate obstacles and non-training solutions; build realistic practice where practice can help; supply necessary information; prototype and test; evaluate performance. This skill adds its **own implementation-specific** evidence statuses, record control, approval gates, mode selection, portable templates, and role handoffs.

## Interpretation rules

- Do not claim that *Map It!* was read in full or that this skill is an authorized adaptation of the book.
- Do not present this skill's proprietary-looking gates, templates, or ratings as Cathy Moore's exact framework.
- The skill is a decision aid: it does not replace stakeholder validation, organization's governance, or human learning-design judgement.
