# Write feedback questions for training content

**Audience:** Workplace

## Purpose

Draft low-effort questions for comments, polls, or replies that can guide revisions and future training topics.

## When to use it

Use this when adding a feedback question to an employee tip, professional video, or training follow-up.

## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Goal
Write feedback questions that help me decide what to improve or teach next.

Context
Ask [AUDIENCE AND CHANNEL] for a quick answer that feels natural in the channel and avoids requesting sensitive details.

Source
Use [CONTENT AND DECISION] to ground each question in a specific task, step, or outcome.

Expectation
Give five ready-to-use options. Include a short-answer question, a two- or three-option choice, and a question about the step where someone got stuck. For each option, give the expected response format and what I could do with the answers. Recommend one option for the stated decision and provide an optional follow-up that offers useful support. Keep questions neutral and avoid any promise I cannot fulfill.
```

## Customize before running

- `[AUDIENCE AND CHANNEL]` — Who can respond, where they will respond, and how much time they have.
- `[CONTENT AND DECISION]` — The draft or summary of the content and the specific improvement you want responses to inform.

## Example output

*Fictional illustrative excerpt.*

After an example of OneDrive version recovery, ask “Which part would you want to see again: opening version history, choosing the right draft, or confirming the restore?” Use the most selected step to choose the next demo segment.

## Check before using

- Check that people can answer without sharing real filenames, ticket numbers, or confidential examples.
- Choose one question whose answers can reasonably inform the stated decision.
