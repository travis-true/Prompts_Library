# Plan a 90-day content series

**Audience:** Workplace

## Purpose

Build a realistic 90-day plan for workplace tips or professional videos, with a publishing schedule, a baseline, and measurable goals.

## When to use it

Use this before launching a series or when you need to reset the schedule for an existing series.

## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Goal
Plan a 90-day content series that helps [AUDIENCE AND OUTCOME].

Context
My publishing channels and weekly capacity are [CHANNELS AND CAPACITY]. The series can use screen recordings, narration, presenter clips, written tips, or a mix that fits the time available.

Source
Use [EVIDENCE AND BASELINE]. Identify which topics and audience needs the evidence supports. Label any proposed topics that still need validation.

Expectation
Return three phases with a realistic publishing cadence, recurring formats, topic ideas, and a weekly production schedule within my capacity. Detail the first two weeks and give one improvement experiment per phase. Recommend a resource viewers can use after watching. Choose up to three metrics tied to the goal. For each metric, give a definition, measurement source, and weekly review action. Treat targets as planning assumptions. If baseline data is missing, use the first two weeks to establish it before setting numeric targets.
```

## Customize before running

- `[AUDIENCE AND OUTCOME]` — Who the series serves and what people should be able to do after viewing.
- `[CHANNELS AND CAPACITY]` — Where you can publish and the hours you can spend each week.
- `[EVIDENCE AND BASELINE]` — Approved topic backlog, recent coverage, and dated analytics or practice data. Enter “unavailable” for missing metrics.

## Example output

*Fictional illustrative excerpt.*

For a 90-day Microsoft 365 tip series with two hours a week, publish one screen demo and one short Teams follow-up each week. In weeks 1 and 2, record guide opens and successful practice attempts among volunteers who try the task. Use the first two sets of results to pick a realistic target and decide which format to repeat.

## Check before using

- Confirm the combined research, recording, editing, and review time fits the weekly budget.
- Check that each proposed metric has a source and supports a decision.
