# Connect a short video to a useful next step

**Audience:** Workplace

## Purpose

Plan a clear path from a short video to a practice activity, guide, registration page, or professional resource.

## When to use it

Use this when your video should help viewers take a specific action after watching.

## Copy-ready prompt

```text
Goal
Make it easy for viewers to take one useful action after watching my video.

Context
The audience, channel, and intended next action are [AUDIENCE, CHANNEL, AND ACTION]. Keep the wording direct and appropriate for the audience.

Source
Use [VIDEO AND DESTINATION DETAILS]. Use only approved resources and claims. Mark missing destinations or access details for confirmation.

Expectation
Map the shortest workable path from video to destination to completion. Draft the spoken or on-screen call to action, the relevant post, pinned comment, or profile copy, and the destination headline and action label. Add a follow-up only when needed. Identify one way to see whether viewers reached or completed the intended action. Flag unverified link placement, sign-in, or access requirements.
```

## Customize before running

- `[AUDIENCE, CHANNEL, AND ACTION]` — Who the video reaches, where it appears, and what viewers should do next.
- `[VIDEO AND DESTINATION DETAILS]` — The script, verified destination, access requirements, and any approved follow-up or measurement options.

## Example output

*Fictional illustrative excerpt.*

End a Teams clip with “Try it with the sample file in the linked practice guide.” The guide opens with the exercise, shows what a successful result looks like, and includes a quick self-check.

## Check before using

- Try the full path with the intended audience’s access level.
- Verify that the destination delivers exactly what the invitation promises.
