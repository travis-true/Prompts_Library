# Weekly priorities and capacity plan

**Audience:** Workplace

## Purpose

Balance priorities with actual time available.

## When to use it

Identify dependencies, choose the top three outcomes, schedule focused blocks, and reserve [buffer] for interruptions. Show conflicts and proposed deferrals. Do not schedule beyond capacity.
