# AI Email Prompt Pack

**Audience:** Workplace

> Replace bracketed text, add the authoritative source, and verify every important detail before sending.


## 1. Summarize a thread

Summarize this email thread. Return the key points, decisions, action items with owners and dates, open questions, and risks. Mark missing owners or dates **Not specified**.

## 2. Draft a professional reply

Draft a concise reply that addresses every question, states the current decision or status first, and ends with the next action, owner, and timing. Do not invent commitments.

## 3. Write a thank-you

Write a warm, specific thank-you message recognizing \\\[contribution\\\], its impact on \\\[person or outcome\\\], and one authentic detail from the source.

## 4. Send a reminder

Draft a respectful reminder about \\\[task\\\] due \\\[date\\\]. Restate why it matters, what is needed, and where to get help.

## 5. Request information

Draft an information request that explains the purpose, lists the exact items needed, provides the preferred format, and states the response date.

## 6. Share a decision

Draft a decision announcement that states what was decided, why, who is affected, what changes, what stays the same, required actions, and support contacts.

## 7. Escalate an issue

Draft a factual escalation. Separate confirmed facts, impact, actions already taken, current blockers, decision needed, and requested timing. Avoid blame and unsupported conclusions.

## 8. Brief a leader

Create a brief with purpose, key facts, business impact, decision needed, recommendation, risks, next steps, owners, dates, and unknowns.

## 9. Follow up after no response

Write a polite follow-up that references the original request, explains why the response is needed, asks for a specific action by \\\[date\\\], and offers to clarify.

## 10. Introduce two people

Draft a concise introduction explaining who each person is, why the connection is useful, the shared topic or goal, and the suggested next step.

## Prompt quality check

- [ ] Goal is explicit
- [ ] Relevant context is included
- [ ] Output format and tone are specified
- [ ] Authoritative source is attached or pasted
- [ ] Sensitive information is handled appropriately
- [ ] Names, dates, decisions, and commitments were verified
