# Training Asset Quality Checklist

**Audience:** Workplace

> Use this final check before sharing a job aid, guide, deck, worksheet, or course.


## Usefulness

- [ ] The resource solves a specific work or learning need.
- [ ] The intended audience and use moment are clear.
- [ ] Important actions are easy to find and follow.
- [ ] Examples reflect realistic work.

## Accuracy

- [ ] Claims are supported by current, authoritative sources.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, and recommendations are distinguishable.
- [ ] Product names, steps, labels, and links have been verified.
- [ ] Missing information is flagged rather than invented.

## Accessibility

- [ ] Headings follow a logical order.
- [ ] Text is readable and uses sufficient contrast.
- [ ] Meaning does not depend on color alone.
- [ ] Images, links, tables, and media have accessible labels or alternatives.

## Visual clarity

- [ ] The page has a clear hierarchy.
- [ ] Instructions are concise and scannable.
- [ ] Spacing, alignment, icons, and styles are consistent.
- [ ] Screenshots and examples show only necessary information.

## Release check

- [ ] Restricted or organization-specific information has been removed or approved.
- [ ] The correct file version is attached.
- [ ] The destination and sharing permissions are correct.
- [ ] A final reviewer has completed the resource from the learner’s perspective.
