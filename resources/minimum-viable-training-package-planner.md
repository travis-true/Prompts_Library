## Select the smallest effective package

- [ ] Announcement or launch message — creates awareness and explains why it matters
- [ ] Quick-reference guide or job aid — supports performance at the moment of need
- [ ] Demonstration video or live demo — shows the task in context
- [ ] Knowledge article or reference page — provides durable detail and troubleshooting
- [ ] Practice activity — lets the audience apply the behavior
- [ ] Manager talking points — supports local reinforcement
- [ ] FAQ — resolves predictable questions and misconceptions
